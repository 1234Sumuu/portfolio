CASE STUDY: DOCTOR J ARCHITECTURE

Healthcare AI systems cannot afford downstream downtime, IDOR privacy breaches, or LLM rate-limit saturation. To deploy Doctor J as a reliable clinical diagnostic platform for patients and physicians, I engineered a server-authoritative, fault-tolerant infrastructure:

5-Key Dynamic Rotation:Automated fallback with 60-second cooldown prevents quota exhaustion across Gemini vision endpoints.
Atomic Lua Quotas:Redis-backed concurrency control prevents race conditions and shields upstream APIs under high traffic.
Patient Data Isolation:Google OAuth ID verification with strict owner-scoped session gating eliminates IDOR vulnerabilities.
Server-Authoritative Stream:Idempotent message persistence with Celery background OCR workers ensures zero data loss.
