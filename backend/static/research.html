Computer Vision & Medical AI

Automated Lung Cancer Detection Using Deep Learning Techniques: Multi-Scale 3D CNNs, Radiomics, and Uncertainty Abstention

Sumaiya Akter, et al.

Reported performance of CT nodule-malignancy classifiers is notoriously hard to compare due to differing label definitions, image-level data splits, and limited reliability analysis. In this study, we rigorously investigate under pre-specified hypotheses whether fusing radiomics with a multi-scale 3D CNN improves classification, whether deep ensembles with temperature scaling improve calibration, whether uncertainty-based abstention reduces error, and whether Grad-CAM++ and Integrated Gradients explanations are faithful.

Benchmark Corpus: NoduleMNIST3D benchmark derived from the LIDC-IDRI thoracic CT scan cohort.

Key Empirical Findings:

  • •All competitive models reached AUROC 0.88–0.90 (MSNet3D 0.896, 95% CI 0.853–0.934; gated fusion 0.892; radiomics+SVM 0.884).
  • •Multimodal fusion did NOT measurably outperform the deep-only model (AUROC difference -0.004, p=0.74), proving that added complexity brings no gain over simple radiomics on this benchmark.
Empirical Metrics: MSNet3D AUROC: 0.896 (95% CI: 0.853–0.934); Abstention Accuracy Gain: 85.2% -> 91.5% (p=0.002)

RAG & AI Safety

From Statute to Citizen: A Rights-by-Design Audit of a Bilingual Retrieval-Augmented Legal Assistant for Bangladesh

Sumaiya Akter, et al. (LegalSupportAI Research Team)

Ordinary citizens in Bangladesh navigating sudden tenancy evictions, unpaid wages, or family disputes often lack familiarity with statutory procedures and cannot afford licensed counsel. We evaluate LegalSupportAI (com.aci.legalai), an Android-based legal AI service providing bilingual assistance in Bengali, Banglish, and English across the codified Bangladesh Code. Deploying generative AI for legally uninformed citizens carries severe risks if advice lacks verifiable statutory grounding or compromises confidentiality. We audit the service across six architectural stages: citizen multilingual query, intent/jurisdiction gating, tiered statutory retrieval, user privacy shielding, provenance answering, and transactional erasure.

Benchmark Corpus: Complete codified statutory corpus of Bangladesh (The Bangladesh Code, Vol. I - L), comprising over 1,200 enacted acts and statutory rules.

Key Empirical Findings:

  • •Independent legal practitioners rated fluency at 4.83/5.00 and legal correctness at 4.05/5.00.
  • •Discovered critical evidential grounding gap: 51.4% of statutory answers displayed source citations absent from the generator context window, highlighting phantom grounding.
Empirical Metrics: Fluency: 4.83 / 5.00; Correctness: 4.05 / 5.00; Jurisdictional Safety: 96.2%

Medical AI & LLMs

LLaMA-XR: A Novel Framework for Radiology Report Generation using LLaMA and QLoRA Fine-Tuning

Sumaiya Akter, et al.

Automatically generating comprehensive clinical radiology reports from patient chest X-rays is a pivotal frontier in modern clinical AI. Traditional encoder-decoder frameworks frequently generate clinically inaccurate statements or omit rare pathologies due to token distribution biases. We introduce LLaMA-XR, a parameter-efficient framework leveraging Quantized Low-Rank Adaptation (QLoRA) to fine-tune LLaMA on radiology visual-linguistic feature projections, attaining superior diagnostic fidelity and high factual alignment.

Benchmark Corpus: Evaluated on MIMIC-CXR (377,110 radiographs) and Indiana University Chest X-Ray (IU X-ray) benchmark datasets.

Key Empirical Findings:

  • •Attained statistically significant improvements across Natural Language Generation (NLG) metrics including BLEU-1 to BLEU-4, ROUGE-L, and METEOR.
  • •Achieved an 8.3% relative improvement in Clinical Efficacy (CE F-1) score compared to baseline M2 Transformer Progressive models.
Empirical Metrics: CE F-1: +8.3% improvement; BLEU-4: 0.182; ROUGE-L: 0.384; METEOR: 0.198

Using Deep Learning for Automated Wound Analysis: Segmentation and Classification in Wound Care

Sumaiya Akter, et al.

Chronic wound assessment requires precise boundary segmentation and tissue characterization (granulation, slough, necrotic eschar) to evaluate healing trajectory. We introduce a unified deep learning pipeline that combines convolutional semantic segmentation networks with transfer-learned multi-class classifiers, delivering automated clinical measurement of wound area and tissue composition.

Benchmark Corpus: Curated clinical wound photography dataset with expert dermatologist annotations.

Key Empirical Findings:

  • •Achieved mean Intersection-over-Union (mIoU) of 0.871 on wound boundary segmentation.
  • •Tissue classification accuracy reached 91.4% across slough, granulation, and necrotic tissue.
Empirical Metrics: mIoU: 0.871; Tissue Classification Accuracy: 91.4%; Dice Coefficient: 0.893

ChatGPT and the Future World: Analysis of ChatGPT Benefits and Securities

Sumaiya Akter

This paper presents an analytical examination of the emergence and adoption of Large Language Models, focusing on OpenAI’s ChatGPT. The investigation surveys transformative productivity enhancements across software engineering, academic pedagogy, and corporate automation, while systematically highlighting cybersecurity vulnerabilities, data sovereignty hazards, prompt injection risks, and synthetic misinformation vectors.

Benchmark Corpus: Comparative synthesis of 85+ academic articles and industry technical reports on generative AI safety.

Key Empirical Findings:

  • •Categorized the top 5 operational failure modes in conversational LLM enterprise deployments.
  • •Formulated risk mitigation strategies for API key exposure, token-level data exfiltration, and hallucination propagation in corporate pipelines.
Empirical Metrics: Peer-reviewed journal publication; cited in emerging AI governance surveys.
